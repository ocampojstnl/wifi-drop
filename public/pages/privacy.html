Your files

Privacy & safety

Your files stay yours: they go straight between your own devices and nowhere else.

Home network only

Wi-Fi Drop refuses every device that isn’t on your home network. Nobody on the internet can open it or see your files, even with the address.

Direct, never uploaded

Files go from one device to the other. They’re never uploaded to a server, a cloud, or the computer running Wi-Fi Drop.

Encrypted

Every transfer is encrypted between the two devices, so other devices on the Wi-Fi can’t read it on the way.

You choose who gets it

Share with everyone at home, or only with the devices you pick: the others don’t even see the file. You decide whether they may give access to others.

What’s kept, and for how long
  • Files you share stay in this browser tab’s storage while Wi-Fi Drop is open in it, so they survive moving between pages. Stop sharing, or close the tab, and they’re deleted from the browser and disappear from every device.
  • Files you receive are yours: they’re saved where you choose. On a phone, one waiting for its Save tap is kept in the tab until you save or dismiss it.
  • Your device’s name and a random id (so files shared with you find you again) are remembered in this browser.
  • The computer running Wi-Fi Drop keeps nothing about your files beyond their names and sizes, in memory, while they’re shared.
{{ad3}}
Who can join

Everyone on your home Wi-Fi is welcome, like a party: they just open the address, no invitation needed. That includes guests you’ve given the Wi-Fi password to. Anyone outside your network is turned away.

Because everyone here can see what’s shared with everyone, use Only people I choose for anything private: then only the devices you pick even see the file.

Advertising

Wi-Fi Drop shows ads to keep it free. Ads load from the advertiser’s servers like on any website; they never see your files, which don’t pass through the internet at all.
