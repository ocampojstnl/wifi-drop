Your files

Privacy & safety

Your files never touch a server: not ours, not anyone’s. They go straight from one of your devices to another.

Never uploaded

Files go directly from the device that has them to the device that downloads them. They are never uploaded to this website, a cloud, or any other server, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Only your network

Anyone can open the site, but you only ever see the devices on your own Wi-Fi, and only they see you. Someone on another network can’t see your devices or your files.

Encrypted

Every transfer is encrypted between the two devices, so nobody in between can read it.

You choose who gets it

Share with everyone on your Wi-Fi, or only with the devices you pick: the others don’t even see the file. You decide whether they may give access to others.

What the website does, and doesn’t

The website only helps the devices on your Wi-Fi find each other, in three steps:

  1. It gives your device a secret room code and password for your network. They’re made from your home internet connection’s address, which the site doesn’t keep.
  2. Your device leaves a short “here’s how to reach me” note on a few free public relay servers (Nostr relays). The note is encrypted with your network’s password, so the relays can’t read it, and only devices on your network can.
  3. The devices then connect directly to each other. From there on, everything goes device to device: who’s here, names, the files on offer, and the files themselves.

Files, file names and your device list never go to the website, to the relays, or to any other server.

Like any website, the relays can see the internet address a device connects from, but not what it says or who it talks to. To find a direct path, each device also asks a free public helper (from Google or Cloudflare) what its address looks like from outside; no files are involved.

What’s kept, and for how long
  • Files you share stay in this browser tab’s own storage while Wi-Fi Drop is open in it, so they survive moving between pages and reloading. Stop sharing and they’re deleted from the browser and disappear from every device. Close the tab and they disappear from every device at once; the browser’s copy is deleted the next time Wi-Fi Drop is opened in it.
  • Files you receive are yours: they’re saved where you choose. On a phone, one waiting for its Save tap is kept in the tab until you save or dismiss it.
  • Your device’s name and a random id (so files shared with you find you again) are remembered in this browser only.
  • Nothing about you or your files is stored on the website.

Who can join

Everyone on your Wi-Fi who opens the site, like a party: no invitation needed, guests included. Because everyone here can see what’s shared with everyone, use Only people I choose for anything private.
