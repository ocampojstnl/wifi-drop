How Wi-Fi Drop works

Send photos, videos and documents between every phone, tablet and computer at home, without cables, cloud accounts or uploads.

Step 1
1

Open the site

On any phone, tablet or computer connected to your Wi-Fi, open this website. Nothing to install, no account. Wi-Fi Drop finds the other devices on the same Wi-Fi by itself.

Step 2
2

Everyone joins

Anyone else on the same Wi-Fi opens the site too, or scans the QR code on a device that’s already in. No invitation needed. Give each device a name so the others know who it is.

Step 3
3

Share and download

Drop files on any device and choose who gets them: everyone, or only the people you pick. The others see them on the Devices page and download them straight away.

{{ad2}}
Where your files go

When someone downloads a file, it travels directly from your device to theirs. The website only introduces the two devices to each other, with a few small messages. The file itself never goes to the website or any other server, and is never stored anywhere.

The connection between the two devices is encrypted, like a secure website. And you only ever meet the devices on your own Wi-Fi: people on other networks open the same site but never see you.

Moving between pages

You can browse Wi-Fi Drop’s pages while you share: the files you shared stay available on every page, kept in this browser tab, and reloading the page keeps them too. They stay until you stop sharing them, or close the tab.

Questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, to open the site and find the other devices. The files themselves then go straight between your devices, not over the internet to a server.

Why can’t I see my other device?

Both must be on the same Wi-Fi (not mobile data, and not a VPN, which makes a device look like it’s somewhere else). A private or incognito window counts as a separate device.

Is there a size limit?

Computers can receive files of any size: Chrome and Edge write them straight to disk. Phones hold a file in memory until you save it, so very large videos (over about 1.5 GB) may not fit.

How fast is it?

As fast as your home network allows. A phone close to the router on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is quickest. See Help for tips.

Which browsers work?

Any recent Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox or Samsung Internet, on phones and computers.
