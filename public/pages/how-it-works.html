How Wi-Fi Drop works

Send photos, videos and documents between every phone, tablet and computer at home, without cables, cloud accounts or uploads.

Step 1
1

Open it on a computer

Start Wi-Fi Drop on a PC connected to your home network. Its page shows the address to open on other devices, and a QR code for it. If Wi-Fi Drop is already running on another computer at home, they find each other by themselves.

Step 2
2

Everyone joins

Anyone on the same Wi-Fi, on a phone, tablet or computer, opens the address shown on any device already in, or scans its QR code. No app, no invitation. Give each device a name so the others know who it is.

Step 3
3

Share and download

Drop files on any device and choose who gets them: everyone, or only the people you pick. The others see them on the Devices page and download them straight away.

{{ad2}}
Where your files go

When someone downloads a file, it travels directly from your device to theirs, over your own Wi-Fi. The computer running Wi-Fi Drop only introduces the two devices to each other, with a few small messages: the file itself never passes through it, and is never stored on it.

The connection between the two devices is encrypted, like a secure website. And it stays inside your home: Wi-Fi Drop refuses any device that isn’t on your network.

Moving between pages

You can browse Wi-Fi Drop’s pages while you share: the files you shared stay available on every page, kept in this browser tab. Close the tab and they’re removed from every device and from the browser.

Questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Only your home Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi Drop keeps working even when the internet is down, as long as the router is on.

Is there a size limit?

Computers can receive files of any size: Chrome and Edge write them straight to disk. Phones hold a file in memory until you save it, so very large videos (over about 1.5 GB) may not fit.

How fast is it?

As fast as your home network allows. A phone close to the router on 5 GHz Wi-Fi is quickest. See Help for tips.

Which browsers work?

Any recent Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox or Samsung Internet, on phones and computers.
